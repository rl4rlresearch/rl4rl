# Independent reviewer 2 — revised manuscript

Estimated AISciK Research-track acceptance probability: **83%**.

This evaluator re-reviewed the revised manuscript without being told another
reviewer's result. They judged the paper to be in solid workshop-accept
territory with no material blocker.

They found the prior concerns mostly resolved:

- strong fit with AISciK's focus on the role of AI systems in scientific
  practice;
- exactly eight main-text pages before references and appendices;
- 10-point/11-point body typography, larger headings and tables, and
  horizontal-only table rules;
- calibrated nonrandomized/matched identification language and descriptive
  endpoint framing;
- explicit treatment of mechanism-family coding as coarse, regex-assisted,
  post hoc, and non-definitive;
- an anonymized 29-file supplemental ZIP with MIT license, analysis code,
  derived tables, figures, public-message corpus, and qualitative audit.

Remaining non-blocking weaknesses were the observational design, only 13
block-level replications (three for language modeling), lack of blinded human
coding, and omission of the large raw campaign logs from the compact
supplemental archive.
