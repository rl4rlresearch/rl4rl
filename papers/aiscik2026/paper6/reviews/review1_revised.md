# Independent reviewer 1 — revised manuscript

Estimated AISciK Research-track acceptance probability: **82%**.

The reviewer assessed the revised paper as accept-leaning with strong workshop
fit and no material blocker. They found that the manuscript now treats the AI
research process—not the benchmark winner—as the object of study; clearly
describes the matched but nonrandomized design; identifies the 13 blocks as the
replication units; treats the three-block language-model result as exploratory;
and avoids endpoint-performance overclaiming.

The reviewer independently rechecked the following artifact claims:

- 13 PDF pages with eight main-text pages before references and appendices;
- 6,080 proposals across 52 trajectories (1,600 addition, 4,000
  Fashion-MNIST, and 480 language-model proposals);
- intervention placement only in the challenged conditions at proposals
  divisible by ten;
- 304 matched challenge/control checkpoints and 608 checkpoint opportunities;
- 5,802/6,080 source-reconstructible proposals (95.4%);
- the principal novelty, structural-change, retention, token-cost, cycle-gain,
  population-dispersion, and endpoint-caveat results.

They considered the prior identification, pseudoreplication, and
construct-validity concerns adequately addressed for a workshop paper. The
remaining weaknesses were intrinsic rather than blockers: fixed nonrandom
condition labels, only 13 block replications, coarse post-hoc mechanism-family
labels, manuscript density, and the need for a final bibliographic check.
